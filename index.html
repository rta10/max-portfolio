Medical Triage Handoff Assistant

A healthcare workflow concept designed to make ambulance-to-hospital handoffs faster, clearer, and more consistent. During transport, a paramedic can relay patient information through voice-to-text; the application then organizes that unstructured report into a standardized digital handoff card for emergency department staff to review before arrival.

The workflow is structured around the ATMIST handover framework, capturing Age/Gender, Time, Mechanism or Medical History, Injuries or Illness, Signs and vital trends, and Treatment. The concept also adds an explicit Response to Treatment field so the receiving team can quickly understand whether the patient's condition improved, remained stable, or deteriorated after care.

Beyond the handoff card itself, the prototype explores speech recognition, AI-assisted ATMIST extraction, clinical risk guardrails, encrypted transmission concepts, digital sign-off, audit trails, and interactive simulation cases. Together, these elements demonstrate an end-to-end approach to turning field information into structured, reviewable clinical data.
