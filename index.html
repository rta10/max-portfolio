Medical Triage Handoff Assistant App

This is a project that sits at the exact intersection of healthcare operations, data integrity, and AI-driven safety. Below you will find a link to launch the Medical Triage Handoff simulation that incorporates the information described for this concept.

The Concept:

The "Digital Scribe" for the paramedic.

  • Input: Paramedic uses voice-to-text to relay the ATMIST details during transport.
  • Processing: The AI instantly structures this into a clean, standardized "Digital Handoff Card."
  • Output: The information is presented on a tablet in the ED before the ambulance arrives, allowing the triage nurse to see a "Headlines" summary of the patient.
1. The Systematic Handover Protocol

To ensure accuracy, the industry standard is to use a structured mnemonic like ATMIST or SBAR for a "High-Reliability Handover" protocols between paramedics and Hospital Emergency Departments. As well, the “Handoff” assistant uses Military-Grade AES-256-GCM Encryption: The application now dynamically marshals patient demographics (e.g., Name, Age, Gender) and active clinical vitals into a JSON payload, immediately encrypting it with a unique Initialization Vector.

The "High-Reliability" Handover Checklist:
  • A - Age/Gender: Identify the patient clearly.
  • T - Time: Time of incident vs. time of arrival.
  • M - Mechanism/Medical History: What happened? (e.g., "Fall from height" or "Sudden onset chest pain").
  • I - Injuries/Illness: What is the primary clinical problem?
  • S - Signs: Most recent vital signs (BP, HR, SpO2) and trends (e.g., "BP dropped 20 points since last reading").
  • T - Treatment: What have you done? (e.g., "Administered 324mg Aspirin at 14:00").
  • "The Gap" (Your unique contribution): A specific statement on "Response to Treatment"—did the patient improve, stay the same, or deteriorate?
