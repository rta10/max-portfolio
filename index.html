ABOUT

A portfolio that keeps the work simple.

Not every project needs to be a coded application to be worth presenting well. This layout is designed for work that may live primarily in documents, prompts, concept notes, or external visual examples.

The goal is to give each project enough context and presentation that a viewer understands why it matters, then make the supporting material easy to reach.
